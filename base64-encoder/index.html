FAQ

What is a Base64 encoder?

A Base64 encoder converts binary or text data into an ASCII string using 64 printable characters (A–Z, a–z, 0–9, +, /) plus optional = padding. This page encodes UTF-8 text to Base64 and decodes Base64 back to text in your browser.

Does this Base64 tool send my data to a server?

No. Encoding and decoding run entirely in your browser with TextEncoder/TextDecoder and btoa/atob (or a Uint8Array fallback). Nothing is uploaded or stored on a server.

What is the difference between standard Base64 and Base64URL?

Standard Base64 uses + and / and often = padding. Base64URL (used in JWTs) replaces + with - and / with _, and usually omits padding. This tool can encode or decode either variant.

Why does my Base64 decode fail?

Common causes: wrong alphabet (standard vs URL-safe), missing or extra padding, whitespace or line breaks in the middle of the string, or binary data that is not valid UTF-8 text. This decoder strips whitespace and can restore padding before decoding.

Is Base64 encryption?

No. Base64 is an encoding, not encryption. Anyone can decode it. Do not treat Base64 as a way to hide secrets.
